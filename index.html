Bird Articles

Learn about cockatiels, parakeets, and lovebirds

Cockatiels

Cockatiels are friendly birds known for their crests and whistling sounds. They are popular pets because of their gentle nature.

Parakeets

Parakeets are small, colorful birds that are playful and social. They enjoy interacting with people and other birds.

Lovebirds

Lovebirds are energetic parrots that form strong bonds. They are affectionate and curious pets.
