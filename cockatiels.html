Cockatiels

General Care & Habitat

Cockatiels need a medium-sized cage with perches, toys, and room to stretch their wings.

Specialized Diet

They eat pellets, seeds, leafy greens, and fresh water. Millet is their favorite treat.

Lifespan

10–20 years with good care.

Unique Personalities

They are gentle, curious, and love to whistle and sing.

Do They Make Good Pets?

Yes! They are friendly and great for beginners.

Fun Facts

Cockatiels show emotions with their crest feathers.
