Parakeets (Budgies)

General Care & Habitat

Parakeets need a cage with toys, perches, and space to fly short distances.

Specialized Diet

They eat pellets, seeds, vegetables, and fruits.

Lifespan

They typically live bewteen 7-15 years with proper care.

Unique Personalities

They are playful, talkative, and full of energy.

Do They Make Good Pets?

Yes! They are small, cheerful, and are easy to take care of.

Fun Facts

They can turn their heads around roughly 180 degrees to look behind them.
